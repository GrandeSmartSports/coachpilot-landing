COACHPILOT GAME NIGHTS

One night a week in a local school gym. Kids play classics like capture the flag and tug of war, plus baseball, basketball, and more. Every game builds teamwork, confidence, and social skills. Coach Daniel runs every session.

What it is

REAL GAMES. REAL SKILLS.

Game Nights is a weekly gym program where kids build real skills by playing real games. No tryouts, no bench. Every game is an all-play game, so every kid is in it. Kids practice being teammates: communicating, encouraging each other, winning well, losing well, and trying again. That is where the confidence comes from.

Who it is for

EVERY KID GETS A SPOT

Built for ages 5-14. Younger siblings and teens are welcome, every kid gets a spot in the game.

Not ready for a full group?

SMALL GROUPS AND ONE-ON-ONE

Some kids need to build up before a busy gym feels okay. Coach Daniel runs individual sessions and small group sessions as a ramp toward Game Nights. A month of one-on-one, then a small group, then the full night. Or straight into a small group. Whatever gets the kid there.
