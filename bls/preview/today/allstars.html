Allstars

The Little League All-Star tournament is a competitive post-season competition where All-Star teams from each league compete for a District Championship. It is an opportunity for a higher level of play and competition within the regional Little League system. At the end of the regular season, a select group of coaches and players will be chosen to represent BLS in the regional all stars tournament, the first step towards entering the Little League International Tournament, the most widely known youth sports tournament in the world. The tournaments start at the double-elimination District tournament, and teams must win their way to advance through the International Tournament. The 8 to 11 year old tournaments culminate at the State Level, while the Majors and Junior divisions all conclude at a World Series Tournament. More information on the International Little League Tournament can be found here.

To participate as an All-Star you must: be ages 8–14 (Little League age), have participated in the Spring 2026 season, have played in at least 8 games this spring, and have met boundary requirements for BLS Little League.

How are All Star Player Selected?
Players are selected through a voting process involving our League coaches.  Selection is based on a combination of skill, sportsmanship, attitude, and overall contribution to their team during the regular season.  Being chosen for an All Star team is a significant honor that reflects a player's dedication and performance.

 2026 BLS All Star Selection Process Document 

Important Dates: 
Announcement of Players:
We have a special process of informing players that they have been selected for an All Star team.  We are also restricted by Little League International, as to how soon we can publicly announce the teams. 
- Softball All Star team announcement: After May 16th 
- Baseball All Star team announcement: After May 16th

Required Documents:

        Original copy of Birth certificate
        School Enrollment Form
        Medical Release Form
        Tournament Player Verification Form

Commitment Expectations: 
All Stars is serious commitment for players and families.  Practices typically begin immediately after the announcement and may occur up to 5-6 days a week.  The tournament schedule can include games on the weekends, weekdays, and travel may be required depending how far the team advances.  
We kindly ask families to consider their summer availability and willingness to commit before accepting a spot.  Participation requires full dedication to the team and the schedule.
