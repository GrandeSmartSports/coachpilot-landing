Safety and training

Before you take the field

Every BLS manager, coach and volunteer completes these three steps.

  1. 1

    Register as a volunteer

    In your BLS account, when you register your player or on its own.

    Volunteer page
  2. 2

    Background check

    After you sign up, JDP emails you the background check. Complete it every year.

    Resets yearlyOctober 1
  3. 3

    Abuse Awareness Training

    Through Little League, every year. Sign in with the email you volunteered with. No certificate to send.

    Start the training
  4. Then: clearedReady for spring 2027
