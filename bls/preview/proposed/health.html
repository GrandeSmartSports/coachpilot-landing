Players, coaches, familiesHealth & Safety

A fun andSafe
season

Safety policies, code of conduct, emergency procedures and key contacts, so every player, coach and volunteer has a fun and safe season.

In an emergency

911First, dial 9-1-1.
Then:

  1. Give the dispatcher the field name and location, your phone number and what happened.
  2. Do not hang up until the dispatcher hangs up. Keep caring for the injured person until help arrives.
  3. Send someone to the street to flag down the ambulance or fire engine.

From the BLSLL Safety Manual

Report an injury

Within 24 to 48 hours

Any incident where a player, manager, coach, umpire or volunteer receives medical treatment or first aid is reported to the BLSLL Safety Officer. Use the injury tracking form in the Safety Manual.

For everyone at the field

At the ballpark

Safety Code
  • No alcohol or drugs on the premises at any time.
  • No smoking within twenty feet of the dugouts and concession stands.
  • No playing in the parking lots at any time.
  • No swinging bats or throwing balls in walkways and spectator areas.
  • Stay alert at all times for foul balls and errant throws.
  • Keep field gates closed and secured after players enter or leave.

From the BLSLL Safety Code. The full code is in the Safety Manual.
