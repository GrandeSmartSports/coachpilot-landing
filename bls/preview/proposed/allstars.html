All-Stars

Little LeagueSummer tournament

Represent BLSAll-Stars.

The All-Star tournament is Little League's post-season. After the spring season, a select group of BLS players and coaches is chosen to compete for the District 10 championship.

What parents need to know

The tournament trail

  1. 1
    District 10Double elimination. Every All-Star team starts here.
  2. 2
    State Ages 8 to 11 end hereWin your way through District to get here.
  3. 3
    RegionMajors and Juniors play on.
  4. World SeriesWhere the Majors and Junior divisions finish.
How it works

From the spring season to an All-Star team

  1. 1

    Qualify

    To play as an All-Star, a player must:

    • Be Little League age 8 to 14
    • Play the BLS spring season
    • Play in at least 8 spring games
    • Meet BLS boundary requirements
  2. 2

    Coaches vote

    Players are selected through a voting process involving BLS coaches, based on:

    • Skill
    • Sportsmanship
    • Attitude
    • Team contribution
    2026 selection process (PDF)
  3. 3

    Teams announced

    2026, softball and baseballAfter May 16

    Selected players hear first, through a special process. Little League International limits how soon teams can be announced publicly. In 2026, BLS posted the teams May 20 to 24.

  4. Then: practiceStarts right away

A summer commitment

Before you accept
5 to 6 daysPractice days a week, at most.
Weekdays tooTournament games fall on weekends and weekdays.
TravelDepends on how far the team advances.

Please consider your summer availability before accepting a spot. It takes full dedication to the team and the schedule.
