Welcome, BLS coachesCoach Run Book.

Thank you for volunteering to coach. Coaching takes real time and effort, and we appreciate it. None of this happens without you.

This guide walks you through a BLS season, from getting cleared to returning your gear. Coached with BLS before? Use it as a quick refresher.

Ideas or tips for this guide? Email coaching@blslittleleague.org

Before your first practice

Get cleared

Every head coach, assistant coach and team volunteer gets cleared every year, before working with players. Little League resets clearances every October 1, so a clearance from before Oct 1, 2026 does not count for spring 2027.

  1. 1
    Coach sign-up opening soon

    Volunteer sign-up

    Log in at blslittleleague.org, open your account (profile icon, top right) and choose Volunteer. Sign up for your coaching role, fill out the volunteer application and upload a photo ID. You can also sign up when you register your player.

    If the Volunteer page says "No Programs are Currently Displayed," coach sign-up is not open yet. Check back soon.

    New to BLS?Have three references ready
    Volunteer page
  2. 2

    Background check

    14 daysto answer the background check email

    The league checks your ID and sends your request to JDP, Little League's background check provider. JDP then emails you. Open it, confirm your details and submit.

    • Check your junk folder for the JDP email.
    • JDP asks for your Social Security number.
    • Link expired? Email secretary@blslittleleague.org to have it resent.
  3. 3

    Abuse Awareness Training

    Little League's free online course, about 45 minutes. Take it on or after Oct 1, 2026, with the same email as your BLS account, and pick Bonney Lake Sumner Little League as your associated league. Little League emails you a certificate. Save it.

    Start the training
  4. Then: league reviewThe league reviews and clears you.
Every helper, every year

Only cleared adults coach, run drills or sit in the dugout, including at the first practice. The league does not post a list of cleared volunteers. To check on a helper, or on your own status, email secretary@blslittleleague.org.

1How teams are formedDraft or league-built teams, evaluations, assistants and your roster
Draft divisions

Coaches draft

Baseball: Minors, Majors, 50/70 and Juniors.
Softball: Minors A, Majors and Juniors.

Coaches draft these teams after player evaluations.

League-built teams

The league builds

Baseball Tee Ball, Coach Pitch and AA: teams are built by school first, then by age, so teams are even. Your own child is placed on your team.

Softball Minors B (coach pitch): teams are also set by the league.

Player evaluations

  • Baseball: players League Age 10 and up attend. League Age 9 players with at least one season of player-pitch may attend.
  • Softball: all Majors and Minors A players attend.
  • Players drafted to Majors last season go back to Majors and do not attend.
  • Head coaches in draft divisions attend evaluations.

Head coaches and the draft

Before evaluations, the board sets how many teams each division will have, based on how many players registered. The league president appoints, and the board approves, a head coach for each team. Head coach spots are filled fresh each season, so returning coaches are not automatic. Little League calls the head coach the manager.

Drafts follow the Little League rulebook. Questions about the draft or the draft order: email the League Player Agent at pa@blslittleleague.org.

Assistant coaches

  • Baseball Tee Ball, Coach Pitch and AA: you may request one assistant coach, whose child is also placed on your team. Only the head coach can make this request.
  • Softball Minors B: Confirming for 2027
  • Draft divisions: there are no pre-set assistants. Assistants come from the families of the players you draft. After the draft, you recommend them and the board approves them.
  • Every assistant gets cleared with the same three steps as you.

Your roster

When teams are set, your division's player agent emails you your roster with family contact information. Check it right away, and email your player agent if anything is wrong or missing.

Families in draft divisions hear a day or two after the draft. Baseball Tee Ball, Coach Pitch and AA families usually hear in mid-March. Softball Minors B: Confirming for 2027

4Confirming for 2027UniformsWhat each division wears and how to hand uniforms out

Uniform steps can change from year to year. Questions about your division: uniforms@blslittleleague.org (baseball and softball).

Baseball

Pro team uniforms

Baseball teams wear uniforms based on professional baseball teams.

Softball Majors and Minors A

Custom fitted

Players are sized at evaluations, and each player gets a custom-fitted uniform. You get a link to share with families so they can place their order. The board picks team names before evaluations, based on college softball programs.

Softball Minors B (coach pitch)

Two colors, BLS logo

Uniforms come in two-color patterns with the BLS logo. Once uniforms and rosters arrive, you pick a team name or let your players choose one together.

Coach tip

Handing them out

If your uniforms are numbered by size, #1 is the smallest. Hand them out by size at practice. If a player is absent, hold their uniform until you can check the fit. Players can trade numbers afterward, as long as the sizes still fit.

Safety

Keep every player safe

A quick reference. The full details are in the BLSLL Safety Manual and on the Health and Safety page.

911Call first

In an emergency

  • Give the field name and location, your phone number and what happened.
  • Stay on the line until the dispatcher hangs up.
  • Keep caring for the injured person until help arrives.
  • Send someone to the street to meet the ambulance.

After an injury

Any injury that needs first aid or medical care: report it to the Safety Officer at safety@blslittleleague.org within 24 to 48 hours, using the injury tracking form in the Safety Manual.

Head injuries

Suspect a head injury? Take the player out of the game or practice right away. They return only with written clearance from a licensed health care provider trained in concussion care.

Concussion Coach Handout

Start of the season

  • You get a first aid kit at the start of the season. Bring it to every practice and game, and email safety@blslittleleague.org to restock it.
  • Keep each player's Medical Release Form with your roster.
  • Read the BLS Safety Code (in the Safety Manual) to your players early in the season. Your first practice is a good time. You, your coaches and your players sign it, and your player agent checks that it is done.
  • First aid training: the 2027 requirement and how to complete it are coming soon from the Safety Officer (safety@blslittleleague.org). Coming soon

Every practice and game

  • Walk the field first. Report any hazard to the Safety Officer, the Fields Coordinator and your player agent, even if you fix it. If the field is not safe, do not practice or play on it.
  • Never leave a player alone. Stay until every player has been picked up.

Report suspected abuse. As a Little League volunteer, you are a mandatory reporter under federal law. If you suspect a child is being abused, report it to the proper authorities within 24 hours. The Little League Child Protection Program explains how.
