Volunteer Opportunities

Spring 2027Volunteers

Volunteer with BLSGet in
the game.

BLS is a volunteer league serving Bonney Lake, Sumner and Orting. Pick a role, get cleared, and help kids play baseball and softball this spring.

Ways to help

Where you fit

Manager or coach

Usually 2 to 3 events a week

The board appoints managers and coaches every year. In Tee Ball, Coach Pitch and AA, a manager's own child is placed on their team, and the manager may request one assistant coach.

Coaches Corner

Umpire

Junior and adult umpires

Call BLS baseball and softball games. BLS brought back its Junior Umpire Program in 2025, and junior umpires can earn money or volunteer hours for graduation.

Umpire game sign-up

Team and fields

Smaller time commitment

Be a dugout parent or keep score for your player's team. Or help the new Fields Committee, created Nov 1, 2026. It is recruiting volunteers to clean up, clear weeds, set pegs and get fields and job boxes ready for play.

Help the Fields Committee

Board member

Open seatUmpire in Chief

The board runs the league: player placement, fields, scheduling, safety, sponsors and more. Every seat is voted on each October.

Ask about the open seat
x2

Make your hours count twice. Your employer may donate to BLS for every hour you volunteer. Boeing, Microsoft, Starbucks and MultiCare all match volunteer hours.

Corporate matching
Three steps

Become a cleared volunteer

Every BLS manager, coach, and volunteer completes these three steps. Cleared for spring? You stay cleared for Fall Ball.

  1. 1

    Register

    Register as a volunteer in your BLS account, when you register your player or on its own. Not sure yet? Register and get cleared early anyway.

    Sign up below
  2. 2

    Background check

    Oct 1Little League resets checks

    BLS sends your info to JDP, Little League's check provider. Answer JDP's email so your check can start.

    About the check
  3. 3

    Training

    Finish Little League Abuse Awareness training every year. No certificate to send: use the same email as your BLS account.

    Take the course
  4. Then: clearedReady for spring 2027

Questions? Email info@blslittleleague.org Background checks: secretary@blslittleleague.org
