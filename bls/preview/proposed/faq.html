Frequently Asked Questions

Frequently askedQuestions

Spring 2027 registrationOpens Friday, Nov 27
1When can I register?

Spring 2027Opens Friday, Nov 27. Black Friday and Early Bird discounts run Nov 27 to Dec 31; the windows and fees are on the Registration page.

Refunds for canceled registrations are not given once rosters are released.

Registration

2Which division is my player in?

League age decides, not school grade.

  • BaseballAge on Aug 31, 2027
  • SoftballAge on Dec 31, 2026

Find your league age and division

BLS serves Bonney Lake, Sumner and Orting. Outside the boundary? Email president@blslittleleague.org before you register.

Challenger: adaptive baseball for players with physical and intellectual challenges, ages 4 to 18, or up to 22 if still in school. vpchallenger@blslittleleague.org

3Does my player need to attend evaluations?

Any softball or baseball player who was drafted to Majors the previous season does not attend evaluations and will be placed back in the Majors division.

Baseball

  • Players League Age 10 and up need to attend an evaluation.
  • Players League Age 9 who have played at least one year of kid-pitch baseball may attend evaluations.
  • Players League Age 9 and 10 who do not attend evaluations will not be drafted into Minors/Majors and will be placed in AA.

Softball

  • All players registered for player pitch divisions (Majors and Minors A) must attend an evaluation.
4When will I hear my player's team?

Draft divisions

Minors, Majors, 50/70, Juniors: you should be contacted within a day or two after the draft is completed (mid-February).

Non-draft divisions

AA, Coach Pitch, Tee Ball: you should typically be contacted in the middle of March.

Carpool requests are not part of roster formation; for AA, Coach Pitch and Tee Ball, the one exception needs the League President's approval before you register.

5When is the season, and how much time is it?

Draft divisions

Minors, Majors, 50/70, Juniors: practices can start as early as mid to late February.

Non-draft divisions

AA, Coach Pitch, Tee Ball: practices typically start mid-March to early April.

The regular season typically concludes early June.

Games and practices are typically 2 to 3 events per week, each about 1.5 to 3 hours depending on division. Upper division events are generally longer, and can be more frequent, than lower division events.

6Is there help with the cost?

Yes. Families experiencing financial hardship can request scholarship support by submitting an application to president@blslittleleague.org.

The 2026 Night Out with the Rainiers raffle funded the scholarship program for the 2027 season.

7Where are the fields?

Every BLS field site, with directions, is on the Fields page.
