Summer tournament

All Stars sponsor

$2,000 to $3,000per team

Sponsor a softball or baseball All Stars team.

All Stars teams can compete in the District 10 and Washington State Little League tournaments, and ultimately at the Little League World Series.

10U (ages 8 to 10) and 11U (ages 9 to 11)$2,000per team
12U (ages 10 to 12), Intermediate, Juniors and Seniors$3,000per team

  • Title of "Official Sponsor"
  • Your logo featured on our website, in our newsletter and on social media
  • Free participation at our annual Little League Day event in April
  • A team-signed jersey with your name on the back
