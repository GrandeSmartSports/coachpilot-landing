1How teams are formedDraft or league-built teams, evaluations, assistants and your roster
Draft divisions

Coaches draft

Baseball: Minors, Majors, 50/70 and Juniors.
Softball: Minors A, Majors and Juniors.

Coaches draft these teams after player evaluations.

League-built teams

The league builds

Baseball Tee Ball, Coach Pitch and AA: teams are built by school first, then by age, so teams are even. Your own child is placed on your team.

Softball Minors B (coach pitch): teams are also set by the league.

Player evaluations

  • Baseball: players League Age 10 and up attend. League Age 9 players with at least one season of player-pitch may attend.
  • Softball: all Majors and Minors A players attend.
  • Players drafted to Majors last season go back to Majors and do not attend.
  • Head coaches in draft divisions attend evaluations.

Head coaches and the draft

Head coach spots are filled fresh each season, so returning coaches are not automatic. Little League calls the head coach the manager.

Drafts follow the Little League rulebook. Questions about the draft or the draft order: email the League Player Agent at pa@blslittleleague.org.

Assistant coaches

  • Baseball Tee Ball, Coach Pitch and AA: you may request one assistant coach, whose child is also placed on your team. Only the head coach can make this request.
  • Softball Minors B: Confirming for 2027
  • Draft divisions: there are no pre-set assistants. Assistants come from the families of the players you draft. After the draft, you recommend them and the board approves them.
  • Every assistant gets cleared with the same three steps as you.

Your roster

When teams are set, your division's player agent emails you your roster with family contact information. Check it right away, and email your player agent if anything is wrong or missing.

Families in draft divisions hear a day or two after the draft. Baseball Tee Ball, Coach Pitch and AA families usually hear in mid-March. Softball Minors B: Confirming for 2027

4UniformsWhat each division wears and how to hand uniforms out

Questions about your division: uniforms@blslittleleague.org (baseball and softball).

Baseball

Pro team uniforms

Baseball teams wear uniforms based on professional baseball teams.

Softball Majors and Minors A

Custom fitted

Players are sized at evaluations, and each player gets a custom-fitted uniform. You get a link to share with families so they can place their order.

Softball Minors B (coach pitch)

Two colors, BLS logo

Uniforms come in two-color patterns with the BLS logo. Once uniforms and rosters arrive, you pick a team name or let your players choose one together.

Coach tip

Handing them out

If your uniforms are numbered by size, #1 is the smallest. Hand them out by size at practice. If a player is absent, hold their uniform until you can check the fit. Players can trade numbers afterward, as long as the sizes still fit.

Safety

Keep every player safe

911Emergency: call 911 first. Give the field name and location, stay on the line, and send someone to the street to meet the ambulance.

After an injury

Any injury that needs first aid or medical care: report it to the Safety Officer at safety@blslittleleague.org within 24 to 48 hours, using the injury tracking form in the Safety Manual.

Head injuries

Suspect a head injury? Take the player out of the game or practice right away. They return only with written clearance from a licensed health care provider trained in concussion care.

Concussion Coach Handout

Start of the season

  • You get a first aid kit at the start of the season. Bring it to every practice and game, and email safety@blslittleleague.org to restock it.
  • Keep each player's Medical Release Form with your roster.
  • Read the BLS Safety Code (in the Safety Manual) to your players early in the season. Your first practice is a good time. You, your coaches and your players sign it, and your player agent checks that it is done.

Every practice and game

  • Walk the field first. Report any hazard to the Safety Officer, the Fields Coordinator and your player agent, even if you fix it. If the field is not safe, do not practice or play on it.
  • Never leave a player alone. Stay until every player has been picked up.

Report suspected abuse. As a Little League volunteer, you are a mandatory reporter under federal law. If you suspect a child is being abused, report it to the proper authorities within 24 hours.
