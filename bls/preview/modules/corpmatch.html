Employer guides

BoeingGift Match Program

Employees and retirees · Up to $10,000 a year

Boeing's Gift Match Program can double your charitable contributions and volunteer hours, in any combination, up to $10,000 per year. BLS is a qualified nonprofit for this program.

  1. Read the program rules. Boeing Gift Match guidelines (PDF)
  2. Follow our step-by-step guide to record volunteer hours and the company match. Step-by-step guide (Word)
  3. Submit your match at boeing.yourcause.com and search for Bonney Lake Baseball Association.
Match at boeing.yourcause.com

MicrosoftGIVE and volunteer match

Employees · Two programs · BLS qualifies for both

GIVE campaignYou donate money to a qualifying nonprofit and Microsoft matches it.
Volunteer Connection SystemYou record your BLS volunteer time and Microsoft matches it with a cash gift.

Microsoft match example

  1. On the corporate network, go to the GIVE portal at aka.ms/give.
  2. Choose Donate to give money or request a match of a financial donation. Choose Volunteer and Track time to have volunteer time matched.
  3. Search Bonney Lake Baseball Association and click on Bonney Lake Sumner Little League.
  4. Enter your hours volunteered and the date range, then click Submit.
  5. Please add BLSLL to My favorite causes.

Contact Microsoft for more details on either program.

StarbucksPartner volunteer match

Partners · Matching donations for volunteer time

  1. Go to community.starbucks.com.
  2. Click Sign In as a Partner. You will need your global username and password.
  3. Click My Projects, then the My Partner Match link.
  4. Find our league and choose type = Partner Time.
  5. Select the first one that comes up, the one with our full name on it.
  6. Enter the from and to dates and the number of hours.
