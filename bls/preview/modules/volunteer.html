BLS is a volunteer league serving Bonney Lake, Sumner and Orting. Pick a role, get cleared, and help kids play baseball and softball this spring.

Ways to help

Where you fit

Manager or coach

Usually 2 to 3 events a week

The board appoints managers and coaches every year. In Tee Ball, Coach Pitch and AA, a manager's own child is placed on their team, and the manager may request one assistant coach.

Coaches Corner

Umpire

Junior and adult umpires

Call BLS baseball and softball games. BLS brought back its Junior Umpire Program in 2025, and junior umpires can earn money or volunteer hours for graduation.

Umpire game sign-up

Team and fields

Smaller time commitment

Be a dugout parent or keep score for your player's team. Or join the Fields Committee, which is forming now to keep BLS fields in shape for the 2027 season.

Join the Fields Committee

Open seatUmpire in Chief

The board runs the league: player placement, fields, scheduling, safety, sponsors and more. Every seat is voted on each October.
