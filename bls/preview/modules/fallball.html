How Fall Ball works

A step toward spring

Fall Ball is a developmental season, set up as a progression for next season. Register for the division your player expects to play in next spring.

Weekday practice, Saturday games

Practices start in August. Teams practice during the week and play their games on Saturdays.

Games vs nearby leagues

BLS interlocks with other leagues, so some games are at home and some are at nearby leagues. Most kids really enjoy playing against other leagues.

Coaches and volunteers needed

Fall Ball is a great way to try coaching. Volunteered in the spring? Your background check and Abuse Awareness training still count, so just choose the volunteer sign-up when you register your player.

First time volunteering this calendar year? Complete the background check after you register as a volunteer.

How Fall 2026 ran

Registration opened in July and closed in early August in both 2025 and 2026. Divisions can fill before the deadline, so register early.

  1. Jul 10Registration opensOnline, on the BLS website.
  2. Aug 9Registration closesLimited fields meant some divisions could fill early and go to a waitlist.
  3. Mid-AugPractices beginWeekday practices with your team.
  4. Sep 12Games beginSaturday games at home and at nearby leagues.
  5. Oct 31Final gamesThe last Saturday of the season.
  6. Nov 7Gear returnCoaches return team gear to the Daffodil Shed, 10AM to 1PM.
