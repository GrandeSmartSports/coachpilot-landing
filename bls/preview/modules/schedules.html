Three steps

Follow your team in GameChanger

Follow your player's team to get the game schedule, live and final scores and game alerts on your phone.

  1. 1

    Get the app

    Download GameChanger from the App Store or Google Play. Sign in, or create a free account.

  2. 2

    Follow your team

    Tap the search icon at the top left of the Home tab, type your team's name, select it, then tap Follow Team. The State and City filters help.

    Choose Parent or guardian and pick your player to also get practices and team messages. The coach approves it, or your player's other parent or guardian if they joined first. Got an invite from your coach? Open it to join.

    Team search help
  3. 3

    Sync your calendar

    Open your team, tap the gear icon at the top right, then Schedule Sync and Sync Schedule to Your Calendar. Choose Apple or Google. Google on an iPhone takes a few extra steps, shown in the help below.

Good to know

Before the first pitch

What's free

Following a team, final scores, live game alerts and the live Animated GameStream are free. Live video is free on a limited basis. Box scores and season stats need a paid GameChanger plan.

When games move

When a game moves for rain or a time change, the new time shows on your team's schedule in the app and in your synced calendar. Google Calendar can take up to 24 hours to catch up, so on rainy days, check the app.

Key dates

Registration, Opening Day and other league dates go on the BLS Calendar page as they are set.
