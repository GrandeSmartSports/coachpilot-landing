Three steps

Follow your team in GameChanger

  1. 1

    Get the app

    Download GameChanger, then sign in or create a free account.

    Opens the App Store Opens Google Play
  2. 2

    Follow your team

    Tap the search icon at the top left of the Home tab, type your team's name, select it, then tap Follow Team.

    Choose Parent or guardian and pick your player.

  3. 3

    Sync your calendar

    Open your team, tap the gear icon at the top right, then Schedule Sync and Sync Schedule to Your Calendar. Choose Apple or Google.

  4. Then: game dayScores on your phone
