BLS Little League Fall Ball Program

Fall Ball is set up to be a progression for next season. Players will be playing in the division they would play in for the Spring of 2026.

Registration Link: https://www.blslittleleague.org/Default.aspx?tabid=716841

Practices will begin at the end of August. Games are all on Saturdays beginning on September 6 running through October 25. Teams will practice during the week and play games on Saturdays. We will be interlocking with other leagues. Half of our games will be home and the other half at nearby leagues. Most kids really enjoy playing against other leagues.

Coaches and volunteers are needed. If you have done your background checks for the spring, all you need to do is sign up as a volunteer when you register. If this is your first time volunteering this calendar year, you will need to complete the background check process after registering to be a volunteer.

Fall Ball Age Guidelines

Baseball: Age is based on players age as of August 31, 2026. If your child is currently 8 years old and will turn 9 on or before August 31, 2026, they will be registered as a 9-year-old for Fall Ball.

Co-Ed T-Ball: Ages 4-6
Single A (Coach Pitch): Ages 6-7
AA (Player Pitch Intro): Ages 8-9
AAA (Minors Player Pitch): Ages 10-11
Majors: Ages 11-12
Juniors: Ages 13-14

Softball: Age is based on players age as of January 1, 2026. If your child is currently 8 years old and will turn 9 before January 1, 2026, they will be registered as a 9-year-old for Fall Ball.

Co-Ed T-Ball: Ages 4-6
Minors B (Coach Pitch): Ages 6-8
Minors A (Player Pitch): Ages 8-11
Majors: Ages 11-12
Juniors: Ages 13-14
