BLS Softball Run Book

Minor A

The first year of real fastpitch. Girls pitch to each other, catch a pitched ball, and learn to play the game the way it is played from here up.

Mostly league age 9 and 1011" ball35 ft pitching60 ft bases

Purpose Proposal

Minor A is where a girl becomes a softball player. Every girl should leave the season able to throw, catch, field and hit in a real player-pitch game, and the league should leave the season with more girls who can pitch and catch than it started with.

Winning matters because the girls care about it. It never comes ahead of development or playing time.

The season has three phases District 10 rule

Start of season to Apr 13No walks. At ball 4 the coach steps in and finishes the count. Focus: pitchers throwing strikes without fear, hitters swinging.
Apr 15 to May 5Walks count. The coach steps in only when the bases are loaded and the pitcher throws ball 4. Focus: pitchers working out of trouble, hitters learning the zone.
From May 6No coach pitch. Real softball. Focus: everything together.

Focus skills Proposal

  1. Pitching. Windmill basics: grip, stride, arm circle, follow-through. One pitch, thrown for strikes.
  2. Catching. Receiving a pitched ball, blocking in the dirt, throwing back to the pitcher.
  3. Throwing and fielding. Ready position, ground balls, overhand throw to first, knowing where the force out is.
  4. Hitting live pitching. Stance, load, level swing, tracking the ball, and taking a walk.
  5. Running the bases. Leave when the ball reaches the batter, steal second, run through first.

By the end of the season Proposal

  • Every girl can make a routine throw from shortstop to first.
  • Every girl knows where the force out is before the pitch.
  • Every girl has hit against player pitching, not just coach pitch.
  • Every team has at least 3 girls who can pitch a full inning.
  • Every team has at least 2 girls who can catch a full inning.

Not enough, too much Proposal

Not enough

  • The same 1 or 2 girls pitch every game.
  • A girl who wants to try pitching or catching never gets the chance.
  • Practice is mostly batting practice.
  • No time spent on throwing to first.
  • Girls stand in the outfield all season.

Too much

  • Rebuilding a pitcher's motion mid-season.
  • Teaching spin pitches before the fastball is consistent.
  • More than 3 team practices a week.
  • Coaching every pitch from the dugout during games.
  • A girl caught or pitched past what the rules allow.

A 90-minute practice Proposal

Warm up + throw 15Stations 40Team defense 20Run 15
  • Stations: pitchers and catchers together, infield ground balls, hitting off live or machine pitching. Rotate every 13 minutes.
  • Team defense: force outs, cutoffs, covering bases.
  • Running: leaving on the pitch, stealing second, rundowns.

Game rules that change coaching Rule

  • 3 outs or 10 batters per inning, 5 runs max, including the last inning.
  • Continuous batting order. Every girl plays at least 6 defensive outs.
  • No new inning after 1 hour 45 minutes.
  • No dropped third strike. No stealing while the coach is pitching.
  • Runners leave only when the pitch reaches the batter.
  • Pitching is limited by innings: 12 per day max, and 7 or more needs a day of rest.

Coach's role Proposal

  • Teach one thing at a time and let the girls play the game.
  • Rotate pitchers and catchers on purpose, and tell each girl why she plays where she plays.
  • Tell parents at the first meeting: walks and errors are part of Minor A, and the season gets harder on purpose (the three phases).

For the committee to decide

  • Do we want a minimum number of pitchers per team (3?) and catchers (2?)
  • Should every girl try catching at least once, or only volunteers?
  • Playing time beyond the 6-out minimum. BLS baseball AA requires an inning in the infield and outfield. Do we want the same?
  • A cap on team practices per week?
  • Do we keep standings and playoffs in Minor A, or call it developmental like baseball AA?
  • Pitching at home: do we recommend a weekly pitch limit for practice?
