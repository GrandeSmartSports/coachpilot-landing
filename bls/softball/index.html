BLS Softball Run Book

Minor B

The first year after T-Ball. A coach pitches, every girl bats every inning, and every girl learns to throw, catch, hit and run the bases.

League age 6 to 810" safety ballCoach pitches from 30 to 35 ftUp to 10 fielders

Purpose Proposal

Minor B is where girls fall in love with softball. By the end of the season, every girl can throw to a teammate, catch a soft throw, hit a ball pitched by a coach, and run the bases the right way.

Scores are not reported in Minor B. The win is a girl who wants to come back next year.

How a Minor B game works District 10 rule

  • A coach from the batting team pitches underhand from 30 to 35 ft. The coach can't field the ball.
  • A girl plays the pitcher spot, within 5 ft of the coach, and fields like an infielder.
  • Each batter gets 7 pitches. No walks, no strikeouts, no hit by pitch. If she fouls off the 7th pitch, she keeps batting.
  • The half inning ends at 3 outs, 5 runs, or when every girl has batted.
  • Runners stop when the defense stops the ball. No stealing and no bunting.
  • Up to 10 fielders: no more than 6 in the infield, and outfielders at least 10 ft behind the infielders.
  • No girl sits on the bench more than one inning in a row. No on-deck batter.
  • No new inning after 1 hour 30 minutes. Games run Mar 30 to Jun 1.

2025 District 10 Softball Interlock Rules, Minor B. 2027 dates follow when District 10 publishes them.

Focus skills Proposal

  1. Throwing. Grip across the seams, point the glove at the target, step and throw overhand.
  2. Catching a throw. Two hands, glove open, thumbs together above the waist, pinkies together below it.
  3. Fielding a ground ball. Get in front of it, glove down on the ground, then throw.
  4. Hitting. Feet apart, eyes on the ball, a level swing. Off a tee, a soft toss, then a coach pitching.
  5. Running the bases. Drop the bat, run through first, touch every base, and stop when the ball is stopped.

By the end of the season Proposal

  • Every girl can catch a soft throw with two hands.
  • Every girl can field a ground ball and throw it to first base.
  • Every girl hits the ball on most at-bats against a coach pitching.
  • Every girl runs through first base and knows which base comes next.
  • Every girl has played both infield and outfield in a game.

Not enough, too much Proposal

Not enough

  • Girls stand in long lines and touch the ball a few times a practice.
  • The same girls play infield every game.
  • Practice is mostly batting practice.
  • Nobody shows the girls how to hold a glove or where to throw.

Too much

  • Teaching game situations the way a Majors team would.
  • Long talks while the girls stand still.
  • More than 2 team practices a week.
  • Fixing every part of a swing at once. Pick one thing.

A 75-minute practice Proposal

Warm up + throw 15Stations 35Game play 15Fun finish 10
  • Stations: catching, ground balls, hitting off a tee and soft toss. Groups of 3 or 4. Rotate every 11 or 12 minutes.
  • Game play: running the bases, where to throw after a ground ball.
  • Fun finish: a relay race or a short scrimmage, so every girl leaves smiling.

Coach's role Proposal

  • Keep every girl moving. Small groups, lots of turns with the ball.
  • Praise effort and hustle out loud, by name.
  • Tell parents at the first meeting that Minor B is about learning the game and having fun. Scores are not kept for standings.

For the committee to decide

  • Practice length and a cap on team practices per week?
  • Should every girl play infield at least once per game?
  • Should older Minor B girls try the pitching motion at practice to get ready for AA?
  • How do we place 8-year-olds between Minor B and AA?

AA

The bridge from coach pitch to girls pitching. Girls take their first turns at pitcher and catcher, and a coach steps in so every batter still gets pitches to hit.

League age 8 to 10Coach pitch to girls pitchingBLS division

Purpose Proposal

AA gets a girl ready for Minor A. It is the first time most girls play pitcher and catcher, with a coach there as a safety net. By the end of the season, every girl has batted against a girl pitching and every team has a handful of girls who can pitch to a batter.

AA is a learning division. Development and playing time come first.

Game rules Proposal

BLS has no written softball AA rules yet. The proposal: use the same three phases as BLS baseball AA, so the coach steps out of pitching as the season goes on.

  1. Weeks 1 to 3. No walks. At ball 4, a coach from the batting team finishes the count. No stealing while a coach pitches.
  2. Weeks 4 to 6. Walks count, except with the bases loaded. Then the coach finishes the count.
  3. Week 7 on. No coach pitching. Walks count.
  • When a coach pitches, it is at game speed for this age, not lobbed. The girl in the pitcher spot stays near the circle and can field.
  • Every girl plays at least 4 defensive innings, at least 1 inning in the infield and 1 in the outfield.
  • No girl plays catcher more than 2 innings a game.
  • 5 runs max per inning, except the last inning. No new inning after 1 hour 45 minutes.

Modeled on the 2025 BLS Kid Pitch (AA) baseball rules. Softball pitching distance and ball size for AA are open questions below.

Focus skills Proposal

  1. Pitcher (first time). The windmill motion: grip, stride, arm circle, follow-through. The only goal is throwing strikes.
  2. Catcher (first time). Putting the gear on, getting set behind the plate, catching pitches, throwing the ball back to the pitcher.
  3. Throwing and fielding (every girl). Ground balls, an overhand throw to first, and knowing where the force out is.
  4. Hitting. Swinging at strikes, first against a coach and then against a girl pitching.
  5. Running the bases. Running through first, knowing when to run and when to stop.

By the end of the season Proposal

  • Every girl has tried the pitching motion at practice.
  • Every team has at least 4 girls who can pitch to a batter in a game.
  • Every team has at least 3 girls who have played catcher in a game.
  • Every girl can field a ground ball and throw it to first.
  • Every girl has batted against a girl pitching.

Not enough, too much Proposal

Not enough

  • Only one girl ever tries pitcher.
  • Catchers never practice catching pitches with their gear on.
  • Practice is mostly batting practice.
  • Coaches lob the ball when they pitch in games.

Too much

  • Asking a pitcher for speed before she can throw strikes.
  • One girl pitching for most of a practice.
  • More than 3 team practices a week.
  • Coaching every pitch from the dugout during games.

A 90-minute practice Proposal

Warm up + throw 15Stations 40Team defense 20Run 15
  • Stations: pitchers and catchers working together (any girl who wants a turn), ground balls, hitting off a coach or a machine.
  • Team defense: force outs and throws to first.
  • Running: running through first, rounding a base, stopping.

Coach's role Proposal

  • Give every girl who wants it a turn at pitcher and catcher in practice.
  • Plan who plays pitcher and catcher each game, and tell each girl why she plays where she plays.
  • Tell parents at the first meeting that walks and errors are part of AA, and the season gets harder in steps on purpose.

For the committee to decide

  • Adopt written softball AA rules, using the three phases above?
  • Pitching distance: Little League's Minors distance is 35 ft. Use it all season in AA, or start shorter?
  • Ball size: Little League uses an 11 inch ball for ages 8 to 10. Confirm it for AA?
  • Standings and playoffs, or developmental only like baseball AA?
  • Should every girl try pitcher at least once in practice?

Minor A

The first year the girls pitch to each other. Every girl learns to play the game the way it is played from here up.

Mostly league age 9 and 1011" ball35 ft pitching60 ft bases

Purpose Proposal

Minor A is where a girl becomes a softball player. By the end of the season, every girl can throw, catch, field and hit in a game where a girl is pitching. Every team also finishes with more girls who can play pitcher and catcher than it started with.

Winning matters because the girls care about it. It never comes ahead of development or playing time.

Pitching phases District 10 rule

The season runs in phases to help pitchers develop. In each phase, the coach steps in to pitch a little less.

Start of season to Apr 13No walks. At ball 4 the coach steps in and finishes the count. Focus: pitchers throwing strikes without fear, batters swinging.
Apr 15 to May 5Walks count. The coach steps in only when the bases are loaded and the pitcher throws ball 4. Focus: pitchers working out of trouble, batters learning the strike zone.
From May 6No coach pitch. Real softball. Focus: everything together.

2025 District 10 Softball Interlock Rules, Minor A. 2027 dates follow when District 10 publishes them.

Focus skills Proposal

  1. Pitcher. The windmill motion: grip, stride, arm circle, follow-through. The only goal is throwing strikes.
  2. Catcher. Getting set behind the plate, catching pitches, blocking balls in the dirt, throwing the ball back to the pitcher.
  3. Throwing and fielding (every girl). Ready position, ground balls, an overhand throw to first, knowing where the force out is.
  4. Hitting against a girl pitching. Stance, load, level swing, watching the ball, and knowing when to take a walk.
  5. Running the bases. Leave when the ball reaches the batter, steal second, run through first.

By the end of the season Proposal

  • Every girl can make a routine throw from shortstop to first.
  • Every girl knows where the force out is before the pitch.
  • Every girl has batted against a girl pitching, not just a coach.
  • Every team has at least 3 girls who can play pitcher for a full inning.
  • Every team has at least 2 girls who can play catcher for a full inning.

Not enough, too much Proposal

Not enough

  • The same 1 or 2 girls pitch every inning of every game.
  • A girl who wants to try pitcher or catcher never gets the chance.
  • Practice is mostly batting practice.
  • No time spent on throwing to first.
  • Girls stand in the outfield all season.

Too much

  • Asking a pitcher for speed before she can throw strikes.
  • Fixing everything in a pitcher's motion at once. Pick one thing.
  • More than 3 team practices a week.
  • Coaching every pitch from the dugout during games.
  • A girl pitching more innings than the rules allow.

A 90-minute practice Proposal

Warm up + throw 15Stations 40Team defense 20Run 15
  • Stations: pitchers and catchers working together, infield ground balls, hitting off a pitcher or a machine. Rotate every 13 minutes.
  • Team defense: force outs, cutoffs, covering bases.
  • Running: leaving on the pitch, stealing second, rundowns.

Game rules that change coaching Rule

  • 3 outs or 10 batters per inning, 5 runs max, including the last inning.
  • Continuous batting order. Every girl plays at least 6 defensive outs.
  • No new inning after 1 hour 45 minutes.
  • No dropped third strike. No stealing while the coach is pitching.
  • Runners leave only when the pitch reaches the batter.
  • Pitching is limited by innings: 12 per day max, and 7 or more needs a day of rest.

District 10 Softball Interlock Rules (2025) and the Little League Softball rule book.

Coach's role Proposal

  • Teach one thing at a time and let the girls play the game.
  • Plan who plays pitcher and catcher each game, and tell each girl why she plays where she plays.
  • Tell parents at the first meeting: walks and errors are part of Minor A, and the coach steps out of pitching in phases on purpose.

For the committee to decide

  • Do we want a minimum number of pitchers per team (3?) and catchers (2?)
  • Should every girl try playing catcher at least once, or only volunteers?
  • Playing time beyond the 6-out minimum. BLS baseball AA requires an inning in the infield and outfield. Do we want the same?
  • A cap on team practices per week?
  • Do we keep standings and playoffs in Minor A, or call it developmental like baseball AA?
  • Pitching at home: do we recommend a weekly pitch limit for practice?

Majors

The full game of Little League softball. Girls pitch from 40 feet, runners can leave when the pitch is released, and the dropped third strike is in play.

League age 11 and 1212" ball40 ft pitchingAll-Stars age

Purpose Proposal

Majors is where girls play the full game and learn to play as a team: knowing the situation, talking on defense, and sharing the pitching. Every girl should finish the season better at a position she can play in Juniors.

Winning is part of Majors. Every girl still plays, and no girl's arm is risked to win a game.

Game rules that change coaching Rule

  • Every girl plays at least 6 defensive outs. Continuous batting order.
  • Runners can leave the base when the pitcher releases the ball.
  • The dropped third strike is in play.
  • Courtesy runner for the pitcher or catcher when there are 2 outs.
  • A girl can pitch in up to 12 innings a day. One pitch counts as an inning. 7 or more innings means a day of rest.
  • Up to 3 coaches on the field. A team can play with 8, but the 9th spot in the order is an out.
  • No new inning after 2 hours 30 minutes. Weekday games start at 6:00 pm.

2025 District 10 Softball Interlock Rules, Majors, and the Little League Softball rule book.

Focus skills Proposal

  1. Pitcher. Strikes with the fastball first, then a change-up as the second pitch.
  2. Catcher. Blocking balls in the dirt, throwing to second on a steal, covering home.
  3. Team defense. Bunt coverage, covering bases on a steal, cutoffs and relays, the dropped third strike.
  4. Hitting. A plan for each count, and a sacrifice bunt.
  5. Running the bases. Leaving on the release, stealing, running on a dropped third strike.

By the end of the season Proposal

  • Every team has at least 3 girls who can pitch 2 innings for strikes.
  • Every girl knows her job on a bunt and on a steal.
  • Every girl can put down a sacrifice bunt.
  • Every catcher can throw to second base.
  • Every girl has played a position she could play in Juniors.

Not enough, too much Proposal

Not enough

  • One girl pitches most of the innings all season.
  • No practice time on bunt coverage or steals.
  • Girls play the same single position all season.
  • The strongest 9 play all game and the rest get the bare minimum every time.

Too much

  • Pitching a girl right up to the daily innings limit.
  • Adding pitches before her fastball and change-up are strikes.
  • More than 3 team practices a week.
  • Coaching every pitch from the dugout during games.

A 90-minute practice Proposal

Warm up + throw 15Positions 30Team defense 25Live hitting 20
  • Positions: pitchers and catchers together, infield and outfield work.
  • Team defense: bunts, steals, cutoffs, the dropped third strike.
  • Live hitting: against your own pitchers, with runners on base.

Coach's role Proposal

  • Track every girl's innings pitched and build at least 3 pitchers.
  • Teach the why behind each play so girls make their own decisions.
  • Tell parents at the first meeting how playing time and pitching will work, before the first close game.

For the committee to decide

  • A BLS limit on innings per game for a pitcher, below the rule book?
  • Playing time beyond the 6-out minimum?
  • A cap on team practices per week?
  • How many pitchers should each team have by season's end?

Juniors

The step to middle school and high school softball. Girls pitch from 43 feet and play the full game against teams from across the district.

League age 13 and 1412" ball43 ft pitching

Purpose Proposal

Juniors keeps girls playing softball through middle school and gets them ready for high school softball. BLS had 26 Juniors players on 2 teams last spring, so keeping every girl coming back matters as much as winning.

These girls are the role models for every younger division.

Game rules that change coaching Rule

  • Every girl plays at least 6 defensive outs. Continuous batting order.
  • Courtesy runner for the pitcher or catcher when there are 2 outs.
  • Little League sets no pitching limit in Juniors. The coach manages each pitcher's innings.
  • Up to 3 coaches on the field. A team can play with 8, but the 9th spot in the order is an out.
  • No new inning after 2 hours 30 minutes. Weekday games start at 6:00 pm.

2025 District 10 Softball Interlock Rules, Juniors, and the Little League Softball rule book.

Focus skills Proposal

  1. Pitcher. Command of 2 pitches from 43 ft, and pitching to a plan.
  2. Catcher. Blocking, throwing out runners, and leading the defense.
  3. Team defense. Every situation: bunts, first-and-third, relays, pickoffs.
  4. Hitting. Adjusting to faster pitching, hitting behind runners, bunting and slapping.
  5. Playing more than one position. High school coaches value girls who can play two.

By the end of the season Proposal

  • Every girl can play at least 2 positions.
  • Every team has at least 3 girls who can pitch from 43 ft for strikes.
  • Every girl knows her job in every common situation.
  • Every girl wants to play again next year.

Not enough, too much Proposal

Not enough

  • Girls play only one position.
  • Practices have no plan, just batting practice.
  • Older girls are never asked to lead.

Too much

  • Pitching a girl in league games the same weekend as travel ball without checking her total innings.
  • More than 3 team practices a week.
  • Coaching every pitch from the dugout during games.

A 90-minute practice Proposal

Warm up + throw 15Positions 25Team defense 30Live hitting 20
  • Positions: each girl works a second position.
  • Team defense: situations at game speed.
  • Live hitting: against your own pitchers, with a count and runners on base.

Coach's role Proposal

  • Treat the girls like the older players they are. Ask for their input.
  • Check in with families about travel ball and school schedules, so no girl is overused.
  • Tell parents at the first meeting how playing time and pitching will work.

For the committee to decide

  • How do we keep enough girls for Juniors teams next spring?
  • Should Juniors girls help at a Minor B or AA practice once a season?
  • A cap on team practices per week?
  • Little League sets no pitching limit in Juniors. Should BLS set one, especially for girls who also play travel ball?
