OnDeck for the Web

The walkup music PA system — every capability of the iPhone app, rebuilt to run on any phone. Android, iPhone, tablet: open a link, set up your team, run your PA.

What You Get

  • Your whole roster, player by player — add a player in seconds, free, no limits
  • Pro announcer voice — every name rendered by a studio-grade AI announcer: "Now batting..., number 7, Mia RODRIGUEZ!!"
  • Names said right — a pronunciation system with a 4,500-name dictionary, suggestions from other coaches, or record the name once and the app learns it
  • Walkup songs — browse by vibe (Hype, Pop, Country, Kids...), grab from 40 ready-to-go picks with pre-set start times, or search anything and set the exact start second yourself
  • Game Day mode — big-button PA console: current batter, on-deck, play, next. Tap and coach.
  • Teams — multiple squads, jersey numbers, drag-to-order batting lineup
  • Guided first-time setup — a 3-minute training that walks a new coach through their first player and earns them starter credits

The Look

Identical to the iPhone app: dark, clean, amber on black. Light mode included, follows your phone. This page is dressed in it.
