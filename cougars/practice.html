Once games start September 12, practices drop to Thursdays only. Any weather calls or changes go out in GameChanger chat first.

What to expect each night
TuesdaysFielding night

Primarily fielding: grounders, pop flies, throwing, positions, and game situations. Expect her to come home with dirt on the glove side.

ThursdaysHitting night

Primarily hitting: tee work, soft toss, front toss, and live swings. Expect a tired swing arm and a happy kid.

What she needs every practice
  • Glove
  • Batting helmet
  • Bat, if she has one. We have team bats to share.
  • Water bottle, a big one
  • Rubber cleats or turf shoes. No metal cleats on the school fields.
  • Layers for the weather. Washington evenings cool off fast.
