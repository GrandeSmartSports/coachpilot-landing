The Big Ask

Run GameChanger for the team

Every game needs a scorekeeper in the GameChanger app: every pitch, every play, logged as it happens. It is honestly a fun way to learn how to score softball, and you will know the game better than anyone in the stands. But it is a real commitment. It is demanding and takes your full attention for the whole game.

This does not have to be one person. A group of parents splitting the games works great too. We would love parents to own this; if nobody can, a coach will cover it. Curious or in, even a little? Put your name below and Coach will reach out. No commitment by raising your hand.

And nobody starts from zero: GameChanger has a free self-paced scorekeeping course. Take a look at GameChanger University.
