🔔 Get Notified

Wish to receive notifications when Coach makes a change or posts a lineup? Add the Cougars app to your phone and enable notifications.

Walk-thru: add the app to your phone
iPhone / iPad (Apple)
  1. Open this page in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button (the square with the arrow).
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  4. Open the new Cougars app from your home screen.
  5. Scroll back to this spot, tap Enable Notifications, then Allow.
Needs iOS 16.4 or newer. On iPhone, notifications only work from the home-screen app, not the browser.
Android
  1. Open this page in Chrome.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu in the top corner.
  3. Tap Add to Home screen (or Install app), then Add.
  4. Open the Cougars app from your home screen.
  5. Tap Enable Notifications, then Allow.
