About your coach

My name is Coach Daniel. My wife Heidi, my daughters Lucy and Lily, and I moved to Washington a year ago in October, and we are still learning what it means to play ball in the wet and the cold. We are not used to it but we are loving every second of it.

I have coached minors softball for the past 4 seasons, 3 down in Arizona and the last 1 here in Washington, and I have been coaching my daughters' sports teams since they were 4 years old. Girls soccer, basketball, and volleyball also, all at the rec level. It is my favorite place to coach, because this is where kids fall in love with sports.

I am high energy and I focus on the positive. We may win, we may not, and that is ok, because at this age we are focusing on building the skills to be competitive later on. I will put all my focus on making sure that when these girls move up to the next level, where wins and losses actually matter, they are ready to compete.

When I am not on the field, I am behind a computer building software and AI tools. That spills over into my coaching, hopefully in a good way, as you are about to see. Fair warning: I tend to over communicate and post information in several places. If you ever feel like you are missing something, please reach out.

The Cougar Way

We go big. Walk up music for every girl. A dugout that never goes quiet. Parents who show up loud behind them. Yes, it is fall ball. We are going to make it something they remember.

Communication

GameChanger chat is our channel for the day to day. Rain rolling in, somebody forgot a piece of equipment, that goes in GameChanger.

Once a week I will drop a link in GameChanger that opens the Cougars App: lineups, volunteer sign ups, snack sign ups, practice schedules, and notes, all in one place. I am hoping to add individual player notes this season too. This app has been growing for a couple of seasons now, and it will keep getting better as we go.

Attendance

I am really laid back on this one. Life happens, and I am running late 99% of the time myself. Make it when you can. Just let me know ahead of time, especially for games, so I can plan accordingly. You can mark attendance right in the GameChanger app.
