The lineup — solved before you leave home

The old way was the hard way: scrambling to enter the lineup at the field. Here's our way:

1Coach publishes the lineup before game time — it goes out through the Cougars app.
2Your phone pings you — as long as notifications are on in the Cougars app (bottom of the team hub: add the app, tap Enable Notifications).
3Enter it into GameChanger — open the game in GC, set the batting order and positions from the published lineup card before you head to the field.
4At the field: confirm, don't create — check with Coach for any late scratches, adjust the one or two names, done before warmups end.
The lineup card always lives at: the Cougars Game Day app — you'll see every girl's batting spot and position by inning, exactly what Coach published.

Game-day tips & tricks

  • You can fix anything. Tapped the wrong thing? Every play can be edited after the fact. Get the outs and runners right in the moment; polish later.
  • When a play confuses you, just record the result — who's out, who's on base. Perfect scoring is a bonus, not the job.
  • Tag team it. One of you watches the field, the other taps. Trading innings keeps it fun.
  • Charge up. Full battery + a backup pack. Scoring drains phones.
  • Sit where you can see the plate. Behind the backstop beats the bleacher corner.
  • Ask Coach anything between innings. Seriously. Anytime.

Your game-day checklist

  1. Before game day: GC app updated, logged into the team.
  2. Lineup notification arrives before game time → enter it in GC.
  3. Phone charged before the game.
  4. At the field: confirm scratches with Coach, adjust the lineup.
  5. During: score it, edit freely.
  6. After: end the game in GC. Stats sync automatically.
