Divisions

Tap a division to see its teams, standings, schedule, and practice status in one place.

Teams & coaches

Every team, its coach, and the division it plays in. The division name groups teams for schedules and standings, so spell it the same way everywhere.

Fields

Every place your league plays or practices. Tag a field with divisions and the schedule tools only put those divisions on it; no tags means any division may use it.

Games

Schedule a game: pick the division, both teams, the date, field, and times. For interlock games against another league (Auburn LL, Kent LL, and so on) switch the opponent to that league's team and, for road games, type in their venue. If the field is already booked, a team would play twice that day, or the field is not tagged for that division, you get a warning first and can still confirm on purpose.

Season board

Every game on the schedule, week by week. Drag a game to a different day, or use its Move button to see open times that fit with no clashes. Rain out one game with its button, or pick a date and rain out that whole day in one go. Rained out games wait in the makeup tray until you give them a new time. When a game is over, hit Final score to record it. Nothing here emails anyone.

Scores & standings

Standings build themselves from the final scores you enter on the Season board. Wins, losses, ties, and run difference per division. You choose which divisions show their standings on the parent-facing portal; many leagues keep the younger divisions off on purpose.

Practice grid

Who practices where, window by window. Coaches claim and change their own spots on the coach portal; this is the league-wide read of it.

Practice compliance

Schedule windows

A window is a stretch of the season, like "Sept 8 to Oct 31". Coaches claim practice slots inside it. Lock a window to freeze it (view-only). When the season ends, Start next season carries your setup into a fresh one and files the old schedule away.

Umpires

Volunteers who call games. Each umpire has their own PIN and only works the levels you give them. They pick up games and answer offers at the umpire page. Contact info stays here in admin. It is never shown on public pages.

The default is 1, the plate umpire. Set a division to 2 to also staff a base umpire. A single game can be overridden from its Umps button on the season board.

Coaches (Coaches Hub)

Head coaches with their own email and 4-digit PIN. When you add a coach, an invite is emailed automatically; the coach clicks the link, sets their PIN, and can then sign in at the Coaches Hub. Every request they submit is tagged with their name so nothing gets lost or fudged.

Bulk import coaches — Download the coaches template (CSV), fill it in (columns: name, email, division, team, phone), then drop it here. Teams are matched by name + division so the same team name in different divisions stays separate. New teams are auto-created if not found.

Tap to choose a file, or drag a .csv or .xlsx here

Board contacts

The board directory a signed-in coach can reach on their hub. Sort order groups related roles together (Presidents 10-19, Player Agents 20-29, and so on). Keeping this current means fewer 11pm phone calls.

Bulk import board contacts — Download the board template (CSV), fill it in (columns: name, role, email, phone, notes), then drop it here.

Requests inbox

Everything a coach files from the hub. Click a request to view it, assign it to a board member, respond, and set its status. Marking a request Resolved or Closed emails the coach with your response.

League announcements

Post a notice to the top of the coach portal, for example a rainy day warning. Posting never emails anyone. Emailing is a separate button with a confirm step.

Set up your league

New here, or starting fresh? The wizard walks the whole setup one small step at a time: league name, season dates, teams, fields, practices, games. Every step can be skipped and done later.

Interlock opponents (other leagues)

Teams from other leagues that we play interlock games against, like Auburn LL or Kent LL. They live on their own list: they never show up in coach pickers, practice claiming, or compliance. Give each one the division name it matches on our side so the season generator can build the right pool.

Season generator

Build a whole season of games in one shot. Set up each division, hit Generate, and the games land as drafts. Drafts are schedule ideas only you can see; coaches and parents see nothing until you hit Publish. Publishing never emails anyone.

Not sure where to begin? Pick a template and every division below gets filled in with a typical setup. You can change anything afterward.

Turn a division on, then set how many games each team plays, how long games run, which days have game times (comma separated, like 10:00, 12:30, 15:00), and which fields it may use.

Import from Excel

Already keep the league in a spreadsheet? Drop the .xlsx file here. Practice-schedule tabs and coach-contact tabs are found automatically, and new fields, teams, and coaches are created as needed. Nothing imports until you review each tab and press its Import button.
