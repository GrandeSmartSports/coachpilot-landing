Games

Schedule a game: pick the division, both teams, the date, field, and times. For interlock games against another league (Auburn LL, Kent LL, and so on) switch the opponent to that league's team and, for road games, type in their venue. If the field is already booked, a team would play twice that day, or the field is not tagged for that division, you get a warning first and can still confirm on purpose.

Interlock opponents (other leagues)

Teams from other leagues that we play interlock games against, like Auburn LL or Kent LL. They live on their own list: they never show up in coach pickers, practice claiming, or compliance. Give each one the division name it matches on our side so the season generator can build the right pool.

Season generator

Build a whole season of games in one shot. Set up each division, hit Generate, and the games land as drafts that only you can see. Review the drafts, fix anything by hand, then Publish to put them on the coach portal. Publishing never emails anyone.

Turn a division on, then set how many games each team plays, how long games run, which days have game times (comma separated, like 10:00, 12:30, 15:00), and which fields it may use.

Season board

Every game on the schedule, week by week. Drag a game to a different day, or use its Move button to see open times that fit with no clashes. Rain out one game with its button, or pick a date and rain out that whole day in one go. Rained out games wait in the makeup tray until you give them a new time. Nothing here emails anyone.
