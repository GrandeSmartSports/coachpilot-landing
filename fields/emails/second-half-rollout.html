Fall 2026 Field Scheduler

Coaches,

BLS has a new online field scheduler that replaces the shared spreadsheet. It shows every available practice field, your practice schedule, and the game schedule in one place.

OPEN THE FIELD SCHEDULER

Getting in, first time only:

  1. Open the link and enter your email, the one the league has on file
  2. Tap the sign-in link we email you (check spam if you do not see it)
  3. Set your own 4 digit PIN. After that your phone stays signed in.

What to do:

  • Confirm your practices. The season (Sept 8 to Oct 31) is loaded and your practices carried over, so there is nothing to rebuild. Review your schedule and confirm it.
  • Grab open field time. Practice fields are first come, first served. Open a field and day, then choose whether to take it for the whole season or just that week.
  • Start times: middle school fields start at 6pm. All other fields start at 5pm.

Need help, or something from the board? Use the Submit a Request button in the scheduler. It routes your message to the right person for field issues, equipment, or questions.
