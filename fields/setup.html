Field Command

Getting Started

You're set up. Here's the walkthrough.

Field Command is your league's field scheduler, coaches hub, and umpire portal — one system, one login for admins, self-serve for everyone else. This page walks you through the first 30 minutes.

1Sign into Admin

One login controls the whole system.

Go to coachpilot.org/fields/admin.html and enter PIN 2575. Bookmark it. That is the only login you need — everything below happens from this console.

Change the PIN later? Yes — send Coach Daniel a quick text and he will rotate it to whatever number you want.

2Import Your Schedule

Drag-and-drop your existing Excel or CSV. No re-typing.

In the admin console, open the Schedule Windows panel. Drop your schedule spreadsheet into the import box. The system auto-detects:

  • Field names + numbers
  • Team names + divisions
  • Time slots per date
  • Coach contact info if it's on a second tab

Preview the parse, then hit Import. Fields, teams, and slots populate instantly. Wrong window? Replace or merge — nothing gets stuck.

Practice or games? Field Command handles practice slots today. Game schedules can flow through the same import — the schedule generator is built and available when you're ready to switch off your current game system.

3Add Your Board Contacts

Coaches see this on their hub — no more "who do I text about fields?"

Open the Board Contacts panel in admin. Add each board member with:

  • Name + role (President, VP, Umpire Coordinator, Field Coordinator, etc.)
  • Email + phone (either or both)
  • Any note ("Text before 8pm please")

These appear on every coach's hub landing so nobody has to hunt through group texts to find the right person.

4Invite Your Coaches

Each coach gets their own email + PIN. No shared spreadsheets. No impersonation.

Open the Coaches panel in admin. For each head coach:

  1. Add their name, email, team, and role
  2. Hit Send Invite — the system emails them a magic link
  3. They click the link, pick their own 4-digit PIN, and they're in

From that point on, they sign into their own personal hub at coachpilot.org/fields with their email + PIN. They can:

  • See their team's practice slots
  • Claim open field time (or release slots they can't use)
  • See the board contact directory
  • Submit a request to the board (rain-out, field issue, schedule conflict)
  • Message another coach coach-to-coach (records stored — see below)
Coach-to-coach messages: Coaches can reach out to any other coach through the hub. Messages route as email to the recipient. Records are stored in the system so the league has a paper trail if anything ever gets disputed — you don't have to look at them, they're just there if needed.

5Post Announcements

One place. Every coach sees it. Optional email blast included.

Open the Announcements panel in admin. Type the announcement, set an expiration date if you want, hit Post. It shows on every coach's hub instantly.

Want to also email it out? Hit Email All Coaches — the announcement goes to every invited coach in one blast. Preview first if you want.

6Umpire Portal (Optional)

Self-serve for your umpires. Assign games, they see their schedule, done.

Umpires log in at coachpilot.org/fields/umpire.html with their own email + PIN. From admin you:

  • Add each umpire (name, email, PIN)
  • Set how many umps a game needs
  • Assign umps to games (or let them self-claim from open slots)

They see their upcoming assignments, get email reminders, and can decline with a reason if they can't work.

Not using it yet? No problem. Skip this step. It's here when you're ready.
